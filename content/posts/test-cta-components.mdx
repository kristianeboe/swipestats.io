## The Psychology of First Messages

Beyond photos, the way you start conversations plays a crucial role in dating success. Our message analysis shows:

### Personalized Openers Win

Generic messages like "Hey" or "What's up?" have a **12% response rate**. Meanwhile, personalized messages referencing something specific from their profile achieve a **34% response rate**.

### Timing Matters

The best times to send initial messages:

- **Sunday 6-8 PM**: Highest response rate (41%)
- **Tuesday 7-9 PM**: Second highest (38%)
- **Friday afternoons**: Lowest response rate (18%)

## Common Dating App Mistakes to Avoid

Based on our analysis of failed profiles, here are the top mistakes people make:

### Mistake #1: Using Only Selfies

Profiles with only selfies see **45% fewer matches** than those with a variety of photo types.

### Mistake #2: Neglecting Your Bio

Empty or generic bios reduce your match rate by **28%**. Your bio should tell a story and give potential matches conversation starters.

### Mistake #3: Poor Photo Selection

Using group photos as your main image, or photos where you can't clearly see the person's face, significantly hurts performance.

### Mistake #4: Inconsistent Activity

Sporadic app usage leads to lower visibility in algorithms. Consistent daily activity (even just 10-15 minutes) improves your profile's reach.
