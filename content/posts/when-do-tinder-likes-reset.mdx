## The Truth About "Resetting" Your Account

You might have heard about "resetting" your Tinder account to get a fresh start. While this used to be a popular tactic, Tinder has cracked down on this practice.

Attempting to reset your account by deleting and recreating it can lead to shadowbanning – where your profile becomes invisible to others without your knowledge.

### Why Account Resets Don't Work Anymore

Tinder tracks users through multiple data points:

- Phone number
- Device ID
- IP address
- Facebook/Apple account (if used for login)
- Photos (via image recognition)

You now have to go through steps like getting a new phone or waiting months for Tinder to delete your data if you want a true reset.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do Tinder likes reset at midnight?

For some users, yes. But it varies – some experience rolling 24-hour or 12-hour windows from when they used their last like. Tinder doesn't publicly disclose the exact mechanism.

### How many free likes do you get on Tinder?

Free users typically get 50-100 likes per 12-hour period, though this can vary based on your activity, location, and other factors.

### Can you see when your Tinder likes reset?

Yes, when you run out of likes, Tinder shows a countdown timer indicating when you'll get more likes. This timer varies by user.

### Do Super Likes reset daily?

Yes, free users get 1 Super Like per day. Tinder Plus users get 5 per day, and this resets every 24 hours.

### What happens if I delete Tinder and reinstall it?

Your like count won't reset. Deleting and reinstalling the app doesn't affect your server-side data, and repeatedly doing this can get you shadowbanned.

### Can Tinder Gold users see unlimited likes?

Tinder Gold users can perform unlimited right swipes (likes) and see who has liked them in their "Likes You" queue without limit.

### Does swiping right on everyone work?

No. Tinder's algorithm detects indiscriminate swiping and may reduce your profile's visibility as a result. Be selective for better outcomes.
