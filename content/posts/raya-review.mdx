## How Does Raya Work?

Raya is the Illuminati of dating apps – everyone's heard whispers about it, but few have actually seen it with their own eyes. Launched in 2015, this app has positioned itself as the velvet rope of the digital dating world, where you might swipe right on an Oscar winner before breakfast.

Think of Raya as the dating app equivalent of getting into Berghain – there's a mysterious committee judging your worthiness, your friends can vouch for you (but that's no guarantee), and even if you check all the boxes, you still might not make it past the digital bouncer.

The app requires you to submit an application that includes linking your Instagram account, selecting your best photos, choosing a song that represents you, and writing a compelling reason why you should be accepted. Then comes the waiting game, which can last anywhere from a day to several months – or forever if they decide you're not "Raya material."

### How is Raya different from other dating apps?

If The League is the country club of dating apps, Raya is the private member's club in Soho House that even most Soho House members can't access.

Unlike The League, which focuses on educational and professional credentials, Raya emphasizes creative accomplishment, cultural influence, and Instagram aesthetics. Your LinkedIn profile won't help you here – it's all about your Instagram grid, your creative portfolio, and whether you've been featured in publications people actually read.

Raya also differs in its multi-functionality. The app offers three distinct modes:

- **Dating:** For romantic connections
- **Social:** For making friends (yes, even celebrities need friends)
- **Work:** For professional networking

It's like LinkedIn, Instagram, and Tinder had a baby and sent it to finishing school in Switzerland.

For a comparison with other exclusive dating apps, check out our [The League review](https://swipestats.io/blog/the-league-review) or [Elite Singles review](https://swipestats.io/blog/elite-singles-review-2025).

### Raya's User Base

Raya's user base reads like a who's who of People Magazine. We're talking actors, musicians, models, directors, writers, athletes, and other people whose job titles include words like "creative," "entrepreneur," or "influencer" (but the kind who actually influence things, not just hawk teeth-whitening kits).

The average Raya user is typically between 25-45, works in entertainment, media, fashion, tech, or arts, and has an Instagram following that makes your 2,000 followers look like a rounding error.

Major cities like Los Angeles, New York, London, and Paris have the most active user bases. If you're swiping in Des Moines, you might see the same three profiles for months.

The gender ratio tends to skew male, which is ironic considering how many men complain about this on other dating apps. Turns out when you're competing with movie stars and Grammy winners, the playing field gets a lot more competitive.

## Pros & Cons of Raya

### Pros:

- **Unparalleled match quality:** Where else can you potentially match with someone you've seen on the big screen? The caliber of users is genuinely impressive.
- **Strong privacy features:** Raya takes privacy seriously with screenshot detection, account verification, and a user base that understands the importance of discretion.
- **Multi-mode functionality:** Date, network, or make friends all in one app. It's like a Swiss Army knife for the creative class.
- **Curated experience:** With only 8% acceptance rate, you know everyone has been thoroughly vetted.
- **Beautiful design:** The app is genuinely well-designed, with a slideshow profile format that's more engaging than endless static photos.
- **No fake profiles:** The verification process essentially eliminates bots and catfishers.
- **Cultural cachet:** Let's be honest, just getting in is a flex.

### Cons:

- **Nearly impossible to get in:** With an 8% acceptance rate, it's literally harder to get into Raya than Harvard (which has a 3.4% acceptance rate, but at least they have clear criteria).
- **iOS only:** Android users need not apply. In 2025. Yes, really.
- **Tiny user base:** Even if you get in, the pool is small. In smaller cities, it's microscopic.
- **Emphasis on clout:** Your worth is heavily judged by your Instagram following and media presence, which is... problematic.
- **Expensive for what it offers:** At $9.99-$19.99/month, you're paying premium prices for potentially seeing the same 20 profiles repeatedly.
- **Slow matching:** The small user base means fewer matches, slower conversations, and more waiting.
- **Professional mixing with personal:** Having your work network potentially see your dating profile adds a layer of complexity.
- **Mystery criteria:** Nobody knows exactly why they got accepted or rejected, creating an anxiety-inducing black box system.

## Who Is Raya For?

### Raya is for you if:

- Your Instagram has more followers than your hometown has residents.
- You've been recognized in public, and not just by your mom.
- Your portfolio includes work that's been featured in major publications, galleries, or platforms.
- You regularly attend film festivals, fashion weeks, or industry events that require credentials.
- You value privacy above all else and need a dating app where NDAs are basically implied.
- You're comfortable with the idea of your professional reputation being tied to your dating life.
- You have multiple friends already on Raya who can refer you (and actually will).
- You're patient enough to wait months for approval and don't need instant gratification.

### It's not for you if:

- Your idea of going viral is getting 200 likes on a vacation photo.
- You own an Android phone and refuse to switch to iOS just for a dating app.
- You want a large, diverse dating pool with lots of options.
- You think social media following is a terrible metric for determining someone's worth as a partner.
- You prefer to keep your professional and personal lives completely separate.
- You need regular matches to stay engaged with a dating app.
- You live outside major metropolitan areas.
- You believe love shouldn't require a committee approval and a three-month waiting period.

## Key and Special Features of Raya

### The Application Process

Raya's application process is more mysterious than the recipe for Coca-Cola. You'll need to:

1. Get a referral from an existing member (recommended but not required)
2. Connect your Instagram account (with a substantial following preferred)
3. Select your best photos for a slideshow profile
4. Choose a song that represents you (yes, your profile has a soundtrack)
5. Write a compelling application explaining why you should be accepted
6. Wait for the anonymous committee to review your application

The committee reportedly considers:

- Instagram following and engagement
- Professional accomplishments
- Referrals from existing members
- Overall aesthetic and cultural contribution

### Slideshow Profiles

Unlike static photo profiles, Raya profiles are slideshows set to music. It's like everyone gets their own mini music video. This adds personality but can also feel a bit like you're judging an MTV cribs episode.

### Three Modes

- **Dating Mode:** For romantic connections. Functions like a typical dating app but with better-looking people.
- **Social Mode:** For making friends. Because even influencers get lonely.
- **Work Mode:** For professional networking. LinkedIn but make it aesthetic.

### Screenshot Detection

Raya aggressively polices screenshots. Take one, and you'll get a warning. Take another, and you're out. This keeps celebrities' profiles from becoming gossip blog fodder.

### The Map Feature

See members in different cities when you travel. Perfect for the jet-setting lifestyle Raya members supposedly lead.

## Sign-Up Process & Creating a Profile

1. Download Raya from the iOS App Store (Android users: sorry, not sorry)
2. Create an account with basic information
3. Connect your Instagram account (make sure it's public and impressive)
4. Upload 3-6 photos for your slideshow profile
5. Select a song from Raya's music library to accompany your profile
6. Write your bio and application essay
7. Add referrals if you have them
8. Submit and enter the waiting game
9. Check your email compulsively for weeks or months
10. Either get accepted (congratulations, you're in the 8%) or rejected (join the 92%)

**Pro tip:** Having multiple referrals from existing members who are active on the app significantly improves your chances. Time to call in those favors from your industry friends.

## Raya Costs

Raya's pricing is relatively modest compared to The League, but let's remember you're paying for access to a very small user base:

- **Monthly Membership:** $9.99/month
- **6-Month Membership:** $29.99 (approximately $5/month)
- **Annual Membership:** $39.99 (approximately $3.33/month)

The pricing is refreshingly straightforward – no tiered memberships or premium features. Everyone gets the same experience once they're in.

However, the real cost is the opportunity cost. If you're waiting 6 months for approval, that's 6 months you could have spent on other dating apps actually going on dates.

## How Swipestats Rates Raya

**Overall verdict: 3.0/5**

Raya delivers on its promise of exclusivity and high-quality matches, but its extremely limited user base, iOS-only availability, and emphasis on social media clout over genuine compatibility make it more of a status symbol than a practical dating solution for most people.

### Attractiveness of the users: 5/5

Let's not beat around the bush – Raya users are stunningly attractive. These are people whose job often depends on how they look, and they have the resources to maintain those looks. It's like every profile was shot by Annie Leibovitz.

### User base: 2/5

Quality is exceptional, but quantity is severely lacking. Even in major cities, you'll quickly run through available profiles. In smaller markets, the app is essentially useless. The 8% acceptance rate creates an incredibly small pool.

### Match rate: 2.5/5

The match rate on Raya is surprisingly low given the small user base. Everyone on the app has options, and everyone on the app knows they have options. Even if you match, conversations can be slow because users aren't as active as on mainstream apps.

### Pricing: 3.5/5

The actual subscription cost is reasonable. But when you factor in the limited user base and match rate, the value proposition becomes questionable. You're paying $10/month to swipe through the same 50 profiles repeatedly.

### Privacy: 5/5

Raya's privacy features are industry-leading. Screenshot detection, verified profiles, and a user base that understands discretion make it the gold standard for privacy-conscious daters.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Raya

### How do you get accepted to Raya?

The exact criteria remain mysterious, but key factors include: having a strong Instagram presence, professional accomplishments in creative fields, referrals from active members, and fitting the overall aesthetic and vibe of the community. The acceptance rate is approximately 8%.

### Is Raya really full of celebrities?

Yes. While not every user is a household name, the app has a legitimate celebrity presence including actors, musicians, athletes, and influencers. Don't be surprised to see people you follow on Instagram.

### How long does Raya approval take?

Anywhere from one day to several months, with most applications taking 2-8 weeks. Some people report waiting over a year. There's no status update or expected timeline.

### Can you use Raya on Android?

No. Raya is iOS only and has shown no indication of developing an Android version. This is one of the app's most frustrating limitations.

### Is Raya worth it?

If you're in the 8% who get accepted and live in a major city, Raya can provide access to an interesting pool of creative professionals and potentially high-profile matches. For everyone else, it's an exercise in rejection and FOMO.

### What happens if you screenshot on Raya?

You'll receive a warning notification. If you continue to take screenshots, your account will be permanently banned. The app is serious about protecting user privacy.
